Eruruu Patch 1.1.0 — Blank Spawn Egg Animal Recovery

Release type: feature release / cumulative patch after 1.0.28.

Main feature:
- Adds the reusable Blank Spawn Egg recovery system for NeoBlock RNG/progression locks.
- 9 Chicken Eggs -> 1 Blank Spawn Egg.
- Journeyman Farmers guarantee 10 Emeralds -> 1 Blank Spawn Egg (4 uses/restock), replacing Cookies while preserving Melons.
- Curing/reputation targets 2 Emeralds; this offer has a 2-Emerald minimum before normal demand increases.
- Blank Spawn Egg recipes produce real vanilla Spawn Eggs for Chicken, Sheep (all 16 colors), Cow, Pig, Horse, Donkey, Rabbit, Wolf, Cat, Bee, Fox, Panda, Turtle, Armadillo, Camel, Strider and Hoglin.
- README documents every recovery craft and the special Rabbit/Sheep rules.

Cleanup included in this cumulative release:
- Removes the old experimental crafting-result selector and its networking/mixins/client state.
- Removes the obsolete Short Grass seed-acquisition recipes that were superseded by the modpack's intended progression.
- Keeps Eruruu independent from Polymorph and other recipe-conflict tooling.

Version:
- gradle.properties: 1.1.0
- build-dev.bat fallback: 1.1.0
