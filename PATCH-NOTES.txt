Eruruu Patch 1.2.0 — Charcoal Progression Cleanup

Release type: feature release / cumulative patch after 1.1.0.

Main feature:
- 9 Charcoal -> 1 placeable Charcoal Block.
- 1 Charcoal Block -> 9 Charcoal.
- Charcoal Block burns for 16,000 ticks, matching vanilla Block of Coal's 10x burn-time ratio.
- 9 Charcoal Blocks -> 1 Dense Charcoal (81 Charcoal total).
- Dense Charcoal keeps the legacy eruruu_patch:endless_charcoal registry ID to preserve existing worlds.
- Dense Charcoal remains non-stackable, keeps its glint and its 2,147,483,647 tick burn time.

Cleanup:
- Removes the old 64-item custom recipe serializer.
- Removes deferred post-craft Charcoal consumption from the crafting grid/player inventory.
- Removes the obsolete JEI/EMI Special Crafting category and manual Endless Charcoal representation.
- Normal recipe JSONs now expose all charcoal conversions to the Recipe Book, JEI and EMI automatically.

Pending roadmap item:
- Turtle Spawn Egg must use the pack's Seaweed item instead of minecraft:seagrass once its exact registry ID is confirmed.

Version:
- gradle.properties: 1.2.0
- build-dev.bat fallback: 1.2.0
